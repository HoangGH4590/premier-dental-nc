Google Gemini pulls answers directly from your GBP. This is the single most important thing for AI visibility.

**Action Items:**

   - Albemarle: 1122 North 6th St, Albemarle, NC 28001
   - Charlotte: 2100 N Davidson St, Charlotte, NC 28205

### PRIORITY 2: Review Generation (Ongoing) — CRITICAL FOR GEMINI

Gemini uses review sentiment and keywords to decide which business to recommend. **Quality of review content matters more than quantity.**

**Action Items:**

1. **Create a direct review link** for each location:
   - Go to GBP → Home → "Get more reviews" → Copy link
   - Shorten it (bit.ly or similar)

2. **Ask every patient after their visit** — train front desk staff:
   > "We're so glad you had a great experience! Would you mind sharing a quick Google review? It really helps other patients find us."

3. **Coach patients on WHAT to mention** (don't script, just suggest):
   - The specific treatment they received
   - The doctor who treated them
   - The location (Albemarle or Charlotte)
   - Example: "Dr. Vu did an amazing job with my dental implants at the Albemarle office. The team was professional and I felt no pain."

4. **Respond to EVERY review within 24 hours:**
   - Positive: Thank them, mention the service, invite them back
   - Negative: Apologize, offer to resolve offline, show you care

5. **Goal: 10 new reviews per month** with service-specific keywords

6. **Review platforms to target** (in order of importance):
   - Google (most important for Gemini)
   - Yelp
   - Healthgrades
   - Facebook
